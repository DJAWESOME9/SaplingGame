Grant Admin Lite extra allowance

Add sap gifts, sap removals, or lightning strikes to a shared Lite login for today. Grants add to the remaining allowance and expire at midnight Eastern.

🌱 Daily sap gifts

Base allowance: 100,000 sap for gifts per day, plus any full-admin grants, shared across all tabs and devices. Resets at midnight Eastern; unused sap does not carry over. Gifts arrive at the player's next online check-in.

Daily sap removals

Base allowance: 100,000 sap for removals per day, plus any full-admin grants, shared across all tabs and devices. Resets at midnight Eastern; unused allowance does not carry over. This is separate from the gift allowance. Removals apply at the player's next online check-in.

⚡ Lightning strikes

Base allowance: 3 lightning strikes per day, plus any full-admin grants, shared across all tabs and devices. Resets at midnight Eastern. Each strike queues for the player's next check-in and active play.
